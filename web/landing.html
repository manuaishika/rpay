Detect → diagnose → decide → act → audit

Revenue leaks at three different points — a checkout that stalls, a payment that fails, an invoice that ages — and each needs a different read before it needs an action.

01

Detect & diagnose

Every record is classified by where it leaked — checkout, payment, receivable — and why. Today that label comes from a stand-in feed, not a live classifier.

02

Gate

A hard guardrail filters the menu first — contact hours, DNC, frequency caps, voice budget. Not a suggestion: the agent physically cannot choose a blocked action.

03

Weigh

Every remaining action is priced against its believed odds of getting the money back, discounted by what's already failed this case.

04

Decide & act

An LLM picks the action — or stops, the moment nothing left clears its own cost. No key on hand, it falls back to the plain math and still runs.

05

Audit

Every decision leaves a trail a second, independent function re-checks. The calls it places come back as real Hinglish audio.

The window, the frequency cap and the attempt ceiling are configurable defaults chosen by us — not taken from an RBI or TRAI circular. What the auditor proves is that the agent obeyed the policy it was given.
